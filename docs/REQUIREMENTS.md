# Requirements

SteelOS is a private, Persian RTL steel trading operating system, initially for one company with future commercialization potential.

Confirmed: independent multi-warehouse locations, company-owned and third-party goods, manual and trusted-source pricing, proprietary double-entry accounting with parallel reconciliation.

M1: foundation, authentication design, product catalog, parties, warehouses/locations, pricing intake, audit design. M2/M3: stock movements, trading, accounting. No paid services without approval.
