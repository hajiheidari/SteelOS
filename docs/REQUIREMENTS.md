# Requirements

SteelOS is a Persian-first RTL internal steel operations platform, organization-aware for
future SaaS. M1 scope: runnable local foundation, identity, catalog, CRM, multi-warehouse
master data, pricing intake/approval history, usable dashboard, audits, tests and documentation.

Confirmed: independent hierarchical warehouses; company and third-party legal ownership
separate from physical location; manual and future licensed trustworthy-source prices;
proprietary future double-entry accounting with parallel reconciliation. M1 uses a free local
mock provider contract, no commercial feed, restricted scraping or mandatory paid AI API.

Catalog is extensible by category rules rather than hardcoded steel families. A founder/operator
can define sheet, coil, rebar, beam, profile, pipe or other categories with required dimensions
and fixed units. No automatic unit conversion or undocumented grade equivalence. Party roles
are many-valued and registration IDs deduplicate where known. Iranian legal/tax identity validation
is not invented; official validation is a later reviewed integration.

M1 must not claim editable stock balances, trading execution, accounting production readiness,
AI automation, tax submissions, offline sync or public deployment. Monetary/quantity calculations
use Decimal/NUMERIC. Dates stored UTC and Persian calendar display uses reliable Intl conversion.

Acceptance requires local clean startup/migrations, authentication/tenant isolation, functional
catalog/CRM/warehouse/pricing UI/API, audit, tests, CI success and matching documentation. See
DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md for actual results and blockers. No paid service activation, main changes,
merge, public deployment or legally binding submissions without explicit founder authorization.
